About us.

Connecting communities to technology, digital skills and opportunity since 2004.

Our Story

Founded in Uganda in 2004, Telekom Equator began by providing accessible internet and digital services to communities in Soroti and Mbale, at a time when personal access to computers and the internet remained limited.

Our early centres offered internet browsing, printing, scanning and other essential ICT services, while also responding to a growing need for practical digital skills. Many of our customers were using computers, email and common software applications for the first time.

This naturally led Telekom Equator to expand beyond access to technology and into digital skills development and practical ICT training.

Digital Skills & Training

From its early years, Telekom Equator supported individuals in developing the basic digital skills needed for education, employment and everyday communication.

Training and guidance covered areas such as:

  • Computer and internet fundamentals
  • Email and online communication
  • Microsoft Office applications
  • Document creation and digital productivity
  • Printing, scanning and file management
  • Practical use of ICT tools

Our approach has always been practical and accessible: helping people become more confident and independent users of technology, regardless of their starting level.
